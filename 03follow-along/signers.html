Who Were the Signers?

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous and influential signers of the Declaration of Independence were:

John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress and famous for his enormous, prominent signature. He was a wealthy Massachusetts merchant and major supporter of independence.
Thomas Jefferson
Primary author of the Declaration of Independence. He was only 33 years old when he wrote it and later became the 3rd U.S. president.
Benjamin Franklin
One of the most famous American figures of the era: scientist, inventor, diplomat, and printer. He was the oldest signer at 70.
John Adams
Massachusetts delegate and one of the strongest advocates for independence. He later became the 2nd U.S. president.
Samuel Adams
Revolutionary leader from Massachusetts and cousin of John Adams. He was heavily involved in organizing resistance to British rule.
John Witherspoon
The only active college president to sign the Declaration. He was president of the College of New Jersey (now Princeton) and an influential political and religious figure.
Richard Henry Lee
Introduced the resolution calling for independence in the Continental Congress.
Roger Sherman
One of the five members of the committee that drafted the Declaration of Independence.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
One of the wealthiest men in the colonies and the last surviving signer, dying in 1832.

If you're doing this for a graphic design/history project, the easiest recognizable five to use would probably be Thomas Jefferson, Benjamin Franklin, John Hancock, John Adams, and Samuel Adams.
