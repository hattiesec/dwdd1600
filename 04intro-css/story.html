Our Story

One Wax began with a simple idea: make surf wax more convenient for surfers while being more thoughtful about the environment. After spending countless days at the beach, we noticed how often traditional surf wax would melt inside hot cars, stick to surf bags, and become a messy part of the surfing experience.

How It Started

Our founders wanted to create a product that gave surfers exactly what they needed for one session without having to carry around an entire bar of wax. That idea became One Wax, a single-use surf wax designed to be easy to grab, use, and take with you to the beach.

Our Community

As One Wax grew, we wanted to make sure our company gave back to the places that inspired us. Our team began volunteering at local beach cleanups and community events. Spending time cleaning up beaches helped us understand how important it is to protect the coastlines and ocean environments that surfers enjoy.

Surf Therapy

A portion of the proceeds from One Wax goes toward supporting surf therapy programs. Surf therapy gives people the opportunity to experience the ocean while building confidence, connecting with others, and discovering the positive effects of being active in the water.

Our Mission

We believe surfing should be about enjoying the waves, spending time with your community, and connecting with the ocean. One Wax was created to make that experience a little easier while giving back to the people and places that make surfing possible.
