About Us

One Wax is a single-use surf wax created for surfers who want a simple and convenient way to prepare for their next session. We believe surf products should make life easier without creating unnecessary mess or taking away from the experience of being in the water.

Surf Better. Leave Less Behind.

One Wax started with a problem that many surfers know too well: melted surf wax. Traditional bars can melt inside hot cars, stick to surf bags, and become difficult to carry around. We wanted to create a product that gives surfers exactly what they need for one session without having to carry an entire bar everywhere they go.

Our single-use design is made for convenience. Grab one before heading to the beach, use it during your session, and avoid bringing a messy bar of wax back into your car.

Why One Wax?

  • Convenient for individual surf sessions
  • Helps prevent melted wax from getting inside your car
  • Easy to carry in a surf bag or beach bag
  • Designed with surfers and the environment in mind

Our Mission

At One Wax, we believe surfing should leave you with good memories, not a melted mess. We want to create products that make surfing more convenient while encouraging people to take better care of the beaches and oceans that make the sport possible.
