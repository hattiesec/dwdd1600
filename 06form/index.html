About Us

One Wax is made for surfers who want a simple, convenient way to keep their boards ready for the water.

Our Mission

Traditional surf wax can become messy and difficult to store, especially when it melts inside a hot car or gets left on a board. One Wax was created to make surf wax easier to carry, use, and enjoy.

Our goal is to create a surf wax experience that is convenient for surfers while also being more thoughtful about the environment.

One Wax is designed with the everyday surfer in mind. Whether you are heading to your local break or traveling somewhere new, One Wax makes it easy to keep your board ready.

We also believe in giving back to the surfing community. A portion of proceeds from One Wax supports surf therapy programs that help people experience the physical and mental benefits of being in the water.

Why One Wax?

One Wax combines convenience, surfing, and environmental responsibility in one simple product.

Our goal is to make choosing and carrying surf wax easier while encouraging surfers to think about the impact of the products they use.
