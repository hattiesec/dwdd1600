Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using technology and the internet responsibly, safely, respectfully, and appropriately.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology and the internet.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules that explain how you are allowed to use technology, computers, networks, or the internet at a school, workplace, or other organization.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, images, or work without giving them proper credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork in order to gain an unfair advantage.

What are the rules for AI generated content?

AI can be a helpful tool, but you need to use it honestly, responsibly, and according to your school's rules.
