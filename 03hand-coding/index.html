About Us

Surf Better. Leave Less Behind.

One Wax was created for surfers who want a simple, convenient way to keep their boards ready without leaving behind another half-melted bar of wax. We believe that something as small as surf wax should be designed with both surfers and the environment in mind.

After too many trips to the beach with a bar of surf wax melting in the car, we started thinking about a better solution. Our answer was One Wax — a single-use surf wax designed to give surfers exactly what they need for one session, without having to carry around a messy bar between waves.

Our goal is to make surfing more convenient while reducing the waste and mess that can come with traditional surf wax. Each package is designed with the idea that less can be more: less mess in your car, less forgotten wax on the beach, and a more intentional approach to the products we bring into the ocean.

Why One Wax?

  1. Convenient: Take exactly what you need for your next surf session without carrying a full bar of wax.
  2. No More Melted Wax: Leave the full bar at home instead of finding a puddle of wax in your hot car.
  3. Less Mess: One Wax is designed to keep your surf gear, car, and beach bag cleaner.
  4. Made With Purpose: We care about protecting the beaches and oceans that make surfing possible.

Our Mission

At One Wax, we believe surfing should leave you with memories, not mess. We are committed to creating products that make life easier for surfers while supporting efforts to protect and give back to the communities surrounding the ocean.
