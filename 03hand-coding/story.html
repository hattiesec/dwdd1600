Our Story

How One Wax Began

One Wax began with a simple problem that many surfers know too well: melted surf wax. After spending a summer surfing along the Southern California coast, our founders were tired of finding half-melted bars of wax stuck to the inside of their cars, bags, and other surf gear.

We wanted to create something that was convenient for surfers while also encouraging people to think more carefully about the products they bring to the beach and into the ocean. That idea became One Wax, a single-use surf wax made for one surf session at a time.

Our Community

While developing One Wax, our team began volunteering with local beach cleanup organizations. Spending time cleaning up beaches showed us how important it is to take care of the places where we surf. We wanted One Wax to be more than just another surf product, so giving back became an important part of our company.

Beach Cleanups
Our team volunteers at local beach cleanups to help remove trash and protect the beaches and ocean environments that surfers enjoy.
Surf Therapy
A portion of One Wax proceeds goes toward supporting surf therapy programs that use surfing and time in the ocean to help people build confidence, connect with others, and improve their overall well-being.
Environmental Responsibility
We believe that caring about the ocean should be part of every surf company. We are always looking for ways to make One Wax more convenient while being more thoughtful about our environmental impact.
Our Mission
Our mission is to make surfing easier and less messy while giving back to the ocean and the people who make up the surf community.

Why We Give Back

Surfing has always been about more than catching waves. It can bring people together, create confidence, and provide a sense of connection. That is why we believe some of the proceeds from One Wax should go toward programs that give other people the opportunity to experience the benefits of the ocean.

Every time you use One Wax, you are supporting our goal of creating a more convenient surf experience while helping us give back to the communities and environments that make surfing possible.
