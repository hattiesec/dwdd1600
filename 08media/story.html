Our Story

Made for Convenience

One Wax was created around a simple idea: getting ready to surf should be easy. Traditional wax can melt in a hot car, stick to other belongings, and become messy to store. Our single-use design gives surfers a simple option to bring along for the next session.

Made for the Ocean

Surfing connects us to the ocean and the communities built around it. One Wax is designed with that connection in mind, combining convenience with a commitment to being more thoughtful about the products surfers take with them.

Giving Back

A portion of our proceeds will support surf therapy organizations and programs that use surfing to create positive experiences and opportunities for their communities.

One Wax, One Wave at a Time

Our goal is simple: make preparing for your next wave easier while supporting the surf community that inspires us.
