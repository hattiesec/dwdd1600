Wax Types

The right wax depends on the water you are surfing in. Colder water needs a softer, stickier wax, while warm water needs a harder wax that will not melt off your board.

Wax Hardness by Water Temperature

Wax Type Water Temperature Hardness
Basecoat All temperatures Very hard
Tropical Over 75°F Hard
Warm 68–75°F Medium-hard
Cool 58–67°F Medium-soft
Cold Under 58°F Soft
