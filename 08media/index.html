One Wax: Single-Use Surf Wax

One Wax is a single-use surf wax created with surfers and the environment in mind. We wanted to create a convenient alternative to traditional bars of surf wax while helping reduce some of the mess that comes with storing and carrying wax.

Why One Wax?

Traditional surf wax can melt in your car, get stuck to your belongings, and become difficult to store. One Wax was designed to make getting ready for a surf session simple and convenient.

Our single-use design gives surfers an easy way to bring wax with them without having to carry around a large or messy bar.

Made for Surfers

Surfing is about getting outside, connecting with the ocean, and enjoying the moment. One Wax was designed around that experience. Our goal is to make preparing for your next session easier so you can spend more time doing what you love.

Giving Back

One Wax is about more than just surf wax. A portion of our proceeds will support surf therapy organizations and programs that use surfing to create positive experiences and opportunities for their communities.
